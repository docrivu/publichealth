About

Dr. Rivu Basu

Dr. Rivu Basu is a public health academic in Community Medicine, based in Kolkata, with expertise in biostatistics, epidemiology, health economics, statistical modelling and health-systems research.

This site brings together learning material, calculators, simulations, public education content and interactive tools for students and practitioners.
